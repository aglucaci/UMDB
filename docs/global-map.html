The table lists the same numbers as the map, for keyboard and screen-reader use.

Countries, regions and cities are validated against ISO 3166 and GeoNames before publication; samples whose location could not be validated count only at the level that could (country or none). Site points show reported BioSample coordinates rounded to 0.01° (about 1 km) and are not inferred from place names. Country boundaries: Natural Earth (public domain) via world-atlas.
